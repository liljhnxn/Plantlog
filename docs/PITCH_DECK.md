## 📌 Slide 2: The Problem
1. **Vanishing Plant Care History:** Popular gardening apps store data in proprietary cloud databases. When apps shut down, care logs disappear.
2. **Web3 Over-Complication:** Most green-tech Web3 dApps force unnecessary speculative tokens, NFTs, or subscription models that alienate regular users.
3. **No Verifiable Care Proof:** High-value specimens (bonsai, rare variegated houseplants, orchids) lack verifiable proof of age, care frequency, and growth rate.
4. **Data Privacy & Ads:** Free apps monetize user behavioral data, push ads, and paywall basic reminders.

---

## 📌 Slide 3: The Solution
**PlantLog: An Ultra-Lightweight On-Chain Garden Journal**
* **Permanent Provenance:** All plant profiles, watering events, and growth milestones are recorded directly to the blockchain.
* **Tokenless & Free from Hype:** Zero ERC-20 tokens, zero NFTs. Pure data utility.
* **Serverless Resilience:** Exactly one webpage and exactly one smart contract. No backend server or centralized database that can fail.
* **Lifelong Tracking:** Plant history remains immutable as long as the blockchain exists.

---

## 📌 Slide 4: Key Features & User Experience
* 🪴 **Plant Registration:** Log species, given name, and personal care guidelines.
* 💧 **Watering Records:** Track water volumes (ml) and organic nutrient notes.
* 📏 **Growth Milestones:** Record height measurements (cm) and foliage developments.
* 📜 **Full Activity Timeline:** Chronological timeline modal displaying complete care provenance.
* 🔒 **Owner Security:** Strict smart contract modifiers ensure only the specimen's owner can update or archive logs.
* 🎨 **Calming Aesthetics:** Warm cream, natural moss green, soft linen white, and earthy terracotta.
